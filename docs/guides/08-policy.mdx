## Retry

```rust
use elm_effect::{retry, Effect, Schedule, Runtime};

let exit = Runtime::run(retry(
    || Effect::fail("timeout"),
    Schedule::<&str, u64>::recurs(3),
));
```

`retry` runs again after a typed failure. It does not run again after a defect or an interrupt. `repeat` runs again after success. They are different functions.

## Remote::call

`Remote::new(RemotePolicy::default())` builds the stack. `call` runs one factory through it.

The default retry count is 3 after the first attempt. The backoff starts at 250 ms. The ceiling is 10 s. The attempt timeout is 30 s. The budget is 2 minutes.

The bulkhead allows 8 calls in flight and 32 calls in the queue. The queue wait is 5 s.

`call` admits the call in this order: filter, cache, rate limit, bulkhead. Then it runs attempts. The attempt timeout starts after the bulkhead admits the call. A full bulkhead is `RemoteError::BulkheadFull`. It is a typed error.

Cache stores a success. It does not store a failure unless you set that in the policy.

Do not hedge a call that is not safe to run twice.
